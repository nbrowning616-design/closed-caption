# Closed Caption — 60 new cartoons in the house style

**How to do this:** paste ONE prompt at a time into ChatGPT. When the picture comes back, send it to Claude with the number and rating (for example, "new 14, Family"). Claude adds it to the deck, writes the real caption (you won't see it) and marks the faces.

**Why:** the critic's count was right. About 45 Standard cartoons is roughly 7 games before repeats. These 60 bring the deck from 66 to 126 cartoons. By lobby choice: Family 29 → 54, Standard (Family plus Standard) 45 → 90, Everything 66 → 126.

**Mix:** 25 Family, 20 Standard, 15 After Dark. 12 of the 60 (1 in 5) use a readable sign as a foothold for new players; each entry says so. After Dark stays grown-up but varied: office politics, dating apps, divorce, hangovers, therapy, HR, bad decisions, mild innuendo. Only one After Dark card involves old people, and it isn't about sex.

**What makes a good premise:** one odd situation, someone reacting with a straight face, and a gap where many different captions could go. Nobody in the picture is obviously the one talking.
