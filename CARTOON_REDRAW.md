# Closed Caption — redraw the deck in the house style

**How to do this:** paste ONE prompt at a time into ChatGPT. When the picture comes back, send it to Claude and say the card number (for example, "card 65"). Claude swaps it in and re-marks the faces. If ChatGPT drifts from the scene, attach the old card picture as well and add: "Same scene and people, redrawn in this style."

**House style (your decision, Oct 18 2026):** classic New Yorker. Black ink line with a soft grey wash on white paper. No colour, no comic-book, neon, poster, painterly, 3D, photo, stained glass or tapestry looks.

**Signs:** most cards now tell the joke with a picture, not a printed sign. About 1 card in 5 keeps a sign or a readable detail, so new players get a foothold. Each entry says which.

**Already in the house style, no redraw needed:** cards 85, 88, 96, 102.

**Order:** Part 1 is the colour and off-style cards (the most obvious ones, do these first). Part 2 is the grey-pencil cards: close to the house style, but pencil rather than ink and wash, so they still look like a different set.

**Totals:** 62 redraws (43 in Part 1, 19 in Part 2). 10 keep a sign and 2 keep a readable detail (numbers or a clock). The other 50 have no sign.

Every prompt ends with the same closing sentence. It's already included.
