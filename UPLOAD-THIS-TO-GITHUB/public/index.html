✉ The caption-writing party game

CLOSEDCAPTION

“”

3–8 players · one phone each · 30–60 min

How to play

A cartoon shows up on every phone with no caption. One caption is the real one — the game's own answer. Everyone else is bluffing. Write a caption that could pass for the real thing, then bet on which one it is.

Each round

1
Write. Everyone except the dealer writes a caption before the timer runs out.
2
Seal the envelope. The dealer secretly hides one player's caption in the Closed Caption. About 15% of the time, the app hides the real answer in there instead — and only the dealer knows which.
3
Blind bets. The dealer reads the open captions aloud. Starting on the dealer's left, each player in turn puts 1 or 2 chips on a caption — including the sealed one. Everyone sees every bet.
4
Open it. The Closed Caption is revealed. Anyone still holding a chip places it now — but not on the caption they already backed.
5
The answer. The real caption lights up, points are paid, and the deal moves left.

Scoring (per chip)

Blind bet on the right open caption+2
Blind bet on the Closed Caption, and it's real+3
Late bet (after the reveal) on the right open caption+1
Late bet on the Closed Caption, and it's real+2
Another player bets on your caption+1
🌗 Half-Moon — both chips, blind, on one open caption. Right = +4.
🌕 Shoot the Moon — both chips, blind, on the sealed Closed Caption. Right = +6. The best feeling in the game.
🃏 Dealer's revenge — if not one chip lands on the real caption, the dealer takes +3.

Good to know

  • You may bet on your own caption. It scores nothing — but it might fool the table.
  • Blind bets pay more than late bets. Courage is rewarded.
  • The real caption isn't always the funniest. Sometimes it's just… fine. Be suspicious of perfection.
  • Dealers: hide the strongest bluff to weaken the field, or hide a dud to make the envelope look boring.
