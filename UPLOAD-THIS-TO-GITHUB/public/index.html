CLOSEDCAPTION

Write a fake caption. Bet on the real one.

Privacy

How to play

Everyone gets the same cartoon, no caption. The game knows the real caption. Write a fake that sounds like it, then bet chips on which caption is real.

Each round

1
Everyone writes. The dealer too. Hate the cartoon? Vote to skip it.
2
One goes face-down. The game turns one caption over: the Closed Caption. Usually a player’s. The real one about one round in seven.
3
Blind bets. The dealer reads the captions aloud. Then everyone bets at once, in secret: 1 or 2 chips on any caption, the face-down one too. The chips drop together. The dealer bets too, the same way.
4
The steal. Before the flip, the dealer must steal one chip from another player. Off the real one, its points go to the dealer. Off a fake, the chip’s owner gets +1.
5
The flip. The dealer flips the Closed Caption. Anyone with a chip left puts it down: everyone at once, in secret.
6
The reveal. One caption at a time: who wrote it, who fell for it. The real one comes last. Points are paid and the deal moves left.

Scoring

Good to know

  • The dealer runs the game. Only the dealer’s phone moves it on. Everyone else sees who they’re waiting for.
  • Bravo! Every caption but your own has a Bravo! button. When most of the other players tap it on the same fake, that caption is framed and hangs in The Gallery. At the end of the game, the table votes one Caption of the Night.
  • Most points wins. A tie goes to whoever fooled the most chips. Still level: more captions framed.
  • Your rating. Ranked like chess. Everyone starts at 1,200. Finish above someone and you take points from them. Your phone keeps it.
  • Bluff. Bet on your own caption: it scores nothing, but it might fool the table. The real one isn’t always the funniest.
  • Every caption is tidied the same way, the real one too. Join late and you start level with last place. Tap the cartoon to zoom.
