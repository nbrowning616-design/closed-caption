CLOSEDCAPTION

·3–8 players · a phone each

How to play

Every phone shows the same cartoon with no caption. The game knows the real caption. Everyone writes a fake one that could pass for it — then bets on which caption is real.

Each round

1
Everyone writes. The dealer too. Lock in a caption before the timer runs out. Hate the cartoon? Tap Mulligan — if half the table agrees, it gets swapped.
2
One goes face-down. The game secretly turns one caption face-down — the Closed Caption. Usually it's a player's, but sometimes it's the real one. Nobody knows which.
3
Blind bets. The dealer reads the open captions aloud. Then, one at a time starting on the dealer's left, put 1 or 2 chips on any caption — including the face-down one. Everyone sees every bet. The dealer doesn't bet.
4
The reveal. The Closed Caption flips over. Anyone still holding a chip places it — anywhere, even on the caption they already backed.
5
The steal. The dealer may knock any one chip off the table. Choose wisely. Expect yelling.
6
The answer. The real caption lights up, points are paid, and the deal moves left.

The reveal and badges

  • The reveal goes one caption at a time, at the dealer's pace: who wrote it and who fell for it. Take your time. That's the good part.
  • Badges are rare and each one is a story: Moon Landing 🚀, Three Bagger, Clean Sweep, Great Minds and Pickpocket. Your phone keeps them in your trophy case (menu ☰).
  • Ranks: land 10 rockets (Moon Landings) to make Captain, 25 for Commodore, 50 for Admiral. Your rank shows at every table you join.

The Grand Poobah

Points tell you who bet best. The Grand Poobah is who wrote best: each round, the player whose fake caption drew the most chips from the table gets the hat. At the end of the game, whoever drew the most chips all night is crowned The Grand Poobah — with all the ceremony that deserves. The winner keeps the points. The Poobah keeps the hat.

Frame It and The Gallery

After each answer, tap your favorite caption to frame it — one frame per game, never your own, no points. When two people frame the same caption, it hangs in The Gallery (menu ☰) for good.

Scoring (per chip)

Good to know

  • Blind chips pay more than late chips. Bet blind on the face-down card and then see it's real? Put your second chip on it too.
  • You can bet on your own caption. It scores nothing — but it might fool the table.
  • If your caption is the one turned face-down, you know the real one is out in the open. Keep a straight face.
  • The real caption isn't always the funniest. Sometimes it's just… fine. Be suspicious of perfection.
  • Every caption — the real one too — is tidied the same way: capitals, punctuation, apostrophes. If you misspell something, the game asks before you lock it in.
  • Tap the cartoon any time to see it full-screen and zoom in.
