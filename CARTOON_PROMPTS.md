# Closed Caption — 30 new cartoon prompts (Family and Standard)

Paste one prompt at a time into ChatGPT. Save the picture as the next `card_N.jpg`, then add its line to `CARTOONS`
in game.js (Family = no flag; Standard = `adult: true`) and write the real caption.

Why these exist: Family has 16 cartoons and Standard 18, so a second game night repeats. The current Family set is
mostly grey pencil while After Dark is colour, so the drawing style gives away the rating. These prompts mix
ink, pencil, watercolour, gouache and full colour across BOTH ratings on purpose. They avoid the overused setups
(doctors/clinics, schlubby husbands, old people and sex, weddings).

A good premise for this game: one clear odd thing, a straight-faced person reacting to it, and a gap where many
different captions could go. Nobody in the picture is obviously talking, so players have to decide who's speaking.

**Paste this ending onto every prompt** (it's already included below):
> Single-panel cartoon, square format. No caption, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no written words except the sign described. Any sign must be big, bold and easy to read on a phone screen. Faces should be expressive but deadpan.
